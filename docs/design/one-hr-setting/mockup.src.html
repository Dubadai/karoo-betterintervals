Settings: without "Heart rate or cadence"

Since every spot on the workout screen is set on its own (Workout screen), "Heart rate or cadence" no longer changes the workout screen. It still picked heart rate or cadence for the free ride's second tile and the Workout done screen's fourth number, and it hid Cadence smoothing unless Cadence was picked, though cadence is on the right tile by default. The proposal: the setting goes, Cadence smoothing always shows, and free ride and Workout done keep heart rate (as they are by default today).
