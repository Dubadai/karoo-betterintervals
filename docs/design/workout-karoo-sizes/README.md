# Workout screen with free ride's Karoo sizes

What the screen without a workout taught us (measured, light, px), carried over to the workout
screen in `mockup.html` (drawn on the app's own screens from the field gallery):

| | Free ride (Karoo sizes) | Workout now | Proposal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tiles | 231 wide, 4 gap, ink 8 from the edge | 218 wide, 12 gap, ink 11–14 | 231, 4, 8 |
| Tile labels | 20 caps (15sp) | 17 (13sp) | 20 |
| Tile icons | 22–23 | 19 | 23 |
| Tile numbers | 64, 12 above the bottom | 38 | 41, 12 above the bottom |
| Clock | 37 | 30 | 37 |

The tiles keep their height (101 px), so Next up and the chart keep their space; the numbers are
as big as that allows.
