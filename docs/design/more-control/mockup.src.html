More control over the workout screen

Four spots the rider picks: the top row (one or two values), the line under the watts, and the left and right tiles (tap to switch between the ones switched on). Defaults: heart rate on top, the target under the watts, Lap avg on the left, Cadence on the right. The gear and ⓘ get the same, bigger tap area.
