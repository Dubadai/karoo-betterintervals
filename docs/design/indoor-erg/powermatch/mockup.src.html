Match PM

One On/Off switch, "Match PM", in Settings › Trainer (indoors), under When paused, shown only while ERG is on. On: no fixed percentage. ERG compares the trainer's own watts with the power meter all the time and corrects the target it sends live, so drift that differs across the power band, and a trainer reading differently cold and warm, are both followed. What it learns per power band is kept as the starting point for the next ride, so a jump to a new target lands close at once. The note says what it saw on the last ride. Settings pages are light only.

1 · Off (the default)

2 · On, not used on a ride yet

3 · On, after a ride: working

4 · On, but the Karoo's power is the trainer's
