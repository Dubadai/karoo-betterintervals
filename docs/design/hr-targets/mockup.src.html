Workouts with heart-rate targets

A step with an HR target runs on heart rate. The big number and the gauge show your heart rate against the step's bpm range. Power moves to where heart rate was, at the top. The tiles show lap avg HR and the bpm target, and Next up shows bpm. The chart draws HR steps by bpm in the Karoo's own heart-rate zone colours, from 80 bpm up so the steps don't all look the same height. Drawn on the app's own gauge screen (field gallery).
