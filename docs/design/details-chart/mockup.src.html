Workout details: the picture

Two changes to the workout's picture at the top of the details page: one scale for every workout, so workouts can be compared at a glance, and a tap on a block shows that interval's watts.

One scale for every workout

Now each picture is stretched to its own highest step, so an easy endurance ride looks as hard as a VO2 session, and the FTP line drops out when nothing reaches it. New: the top is always 130 % of FTP, so the FTP line sits at the same height on every workout. Only a workout that goes higher than that (sprints) makes the scale taller, just enough for its highest step, and the FTP line stays in. The picture is 80 dp tall instead of 120.
